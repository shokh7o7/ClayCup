About us

Two hands, one wheel and a lot of clay.

Clay & Cup started in 2019 in a tiny workshop with one pottery wheel. Today we are a team of five makers, but every cup is still shaped, glazed and fired by hand.

12kcups made

Process

How a cup is born

01

Shape

We shape each cup from soft clay on the pottery wheel.

02

Dry and glaze

The cup dries for a week, then we paint it with glaze.

03

Fire

Finally it is fired in the kiln at 1240 degrees.

FAQ

Questions

Are the cups dishwasher safe?

Yes, all ceramic cups are dishwasher and microwave safe. Glass cups are dishwasher safe too.

Is every cup exactly the same?

No. Because they are handmade, each cup is a little different in shape and color.

How long does delivery take?

One to two days in Tashkent and three to five days in other regions.
